Features

Built on the gym floor — and the walk home

Every feature exists because it was needed while training. Nothing in here is there to sell you anything.

New in 2.0

A pedometer that counts what matters

Steps from the phone's own motion sensor, turned into the numbers you actually care about — and a daily goal that's yours to set.

  • Live speed (km/h), distance, calories and active time
  • Daily step goal with suggestions from 5,000 to 20,000
  • Counts all day in the background — app closed, phone locked — with a live counter on the lock screen and a home-screen widget
  • A live clock in Baghdad time to the second, the Hijri date and a countdown to midnight
  • A buzz, a sound and an optional voice coach every 1,000 steps
  • Stride length worked out from your height — no calibration walk

Hit the goal, get the moment

Reach your daily step goal and MAHDI-JYM celebrates with you: five seconds of a figure throwing its arms up, a short fanfare and a buzz. Then it gets out of your way.

  • Once per day — never nagging
  • Respects "reduce motion" and your sound settings

New in 2.0

Body-weight check-ins with proof

Each weigh-in shows your current weight, the previous one and the difference, coloured by whether it moves you toward your goal, on an interactive chart with your target line.

  • Attach a progress photo, kept only on your device and never synced
  • Distance to target at a glance
  • Import body weight from Apple Health exports

New in 2.0

One daily reminder that knows you

Workout and walking reminders are merged into one daily nudge. Pick a fixed time, or let smart time learn it: half an hour before you usually start training.

  • A gentle follow-up an hour later if you haven't trained or walked yet
  • Native notifications on Android, web push when self-hosted

Guided workouts with real progression rules

MAHDI-JYM knows what day it is, opens today's session and pre-fills your weights. It follows a progression rule (linear, Greyskull LP, double progression or added time), set per routine and overridable per exercise, so the weights are already right and every target tells you why.

  • Rest timer, PR detection, supersets, timed sets and cardio
  • Effort per set as RIR or RPE, and the screen stays awake while you train

See every muscle, every week, every year

A body map shaded by how much work each muscle got (and which ones got nothing), plus a GitHub-style heatmap of your whole year of training and estimated 1RM per exercise.

  • 1,324 exercises with animated demos, filterable by the equipment you own
  • Optional self-hosted AI coach, off unless you turn it on

The story

MAHDI-JYM grew out of openGym, a tiny app Duarte Santos built so he could log workouts and weight without a subscription app claiming his training data. MAHDI-JYM keeps that promise and adds the steps between sessions: a pedometer, a daily goal, smarter reminders and honest weigh-ins.
